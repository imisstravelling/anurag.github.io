My doctoral work examines internet shutdowns, analyzing their political, social, and communicative impacts through the lens of the political economy of communication. Areas of interest: internet regulation, media and technology law, digital rights, misinformation, and telecom policy.

Doctoral research

Dissertation — Internet Shutdowns, Misinformation and the Policy Governance of Communication Infrastructure in India

Department of Communication, Media, and Film, University of Calgary (2021–present)

I closely read internet shutdown orders issued by Indian authorities since 2020 and pair that textual record with fieldwork in Kashmir, Manipur, and Haryana. The shutdown orders routinely justify cutting connectivity as a measure to stop rumours and misinformation, yet people on the ground continue to encounter misinformation through other channels — with no reliable way to verify it once the internet is gone.
