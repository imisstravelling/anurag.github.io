I am an Assistant Professor (Sessional) in the Department of Communication & Media Studies at York University. I completed my PhD in the Department of Communication, Media, and Film at the University of Calgary. My research examines the intersection of law, technology, and democracy. Alongside my research, I bring over five years of experience teaching undergraduate courses in communication and media studies. Prior to my doctoral studies, I worked as a technical writer, journalist, and legal researcher. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Law and a Master's in Media and Communication Studies.

Areas of Focus

Teaching

From undergraduate courses to graduate seminars, I teach media, policy, and digital culture at the University of Calgary.

Information Design

Science communication, technical writing, and data visualization for research institutes, engineering firms, and media organizations.

Research

Doctoral research on internet shutdowns, misinformation, and communication governance in India, published in policy and academic outlets.

Institutional Engagement

Active in faculty committees, graduate unions, and student associations advancing equity, labour rights, and an inclusive campus culture.
